When India's Sub-Junior Men's hockey team met Pakistan at the Dhofar University Sports Complex in Muscat, Oman, on July 21, 2026, the inaugural FIH Youth Hockey5s Asian Championship was only one day old. Karan Gautam, a forward from Hockey Madhya Pradesh, scored three goals as India ran out **7–4** winners — a hat-trick that set the tone for an unbeaten campaign ending in a gold medal.

India's men won every match at the tournament, held from July 20 to 25 in Muscat. They beat Bangladesh on the opening day, then defeated Pakistan and Oman in the pool stage before facing Pakistan again in the final. The gold medal also secured India's qualification for the **FIH Youth Hockey5s World Cup**, giving India's under-18 players a second major stage to prepare for.

## From the Hockey Madhya Pradesh Academy to Muscat

Gautam's path to the India squad ran through the Hockey Madhya Pradesh system. He plays for Hockey MP in national championships and had already stood out at the domestic level: five goals in a single match against Delhi at the 15th Hockey India Sub Junior Men National Championship is the kind of performance that earns attention from national selectors.

In April 2026, Hockey India announced his name in the U-18 National Coaching Camp in Bhopal — the SAI training facility that brings together the country's best under-18 players as they prepare for international competitions. The Bhopal camp has become an increasingly important hub in Indian hockey's development pipeline, and players who enter through the Hockey MP system benefit from close access to its coaching staff and infrastructure.

At the Youth Hockey5s Asian Championship, Gautam was one of the team's most productive forwards. His hat-trick against Pakistan in the pool stage came alongside goals from captain Ketan Kushwaha, Rahul Yadav, Prahalad Rajbhar, and Romit Pal, reflecting the depth of India's attacking options across the squad. But three goals in one match against a rival as motivated as Pakistan is not a quiet contribution.

## What the Gold Medal Means for MP Hockey

India's sub-junior hockey development has been producing players who advance into the junior and senior national setups. For Hockey Madhya Pradesh — a programme that General Secretary Lok Bahadur has built into a 29-medal-producing national championship force over sixteen years — having a forward in the squad for an inaugural international championship is evidence that the state's grassroots pipeline is reaching the highest domestic levels and beyond.

The FIH Youth Hockey5s Asian Championship is new enough that its gold medal is not yet storied. But firsts carry their own weight. India won the inaugural edition, Karan Gautam scored in it, and the Hockey Madhya Pradesh system that produced him is now part of that record. For the coaches at the Bhopal SAI centre and the Hockey MP districts that identified him earlier, the result in Muscat is the kind of return that makes the pipeline worth building.
