On 26 September 2026, three Indian women stood together on the podium at the Aichi-Nagoya Sport Shooting Range: Tilottama Sen, Vidarsa Vinod, and Ashi Chouksey. Their combined score of **1,763 points** placed India in silver position in the women's 50m rifle three-positions team event — just ten behind China's Games-record **1,773**. For Chouksey, it was a medal she knew how to earn.

The 24-year-old from Bhopal had been here before. At the previous edition, Hangzhou 2022 (held in 2023), she had carried home three medals: a silver in the 10m air rifle team event, a silver in the women's 50m rifle 3P team, and an individual bronze in the same discipline — a bronze that nearly became silver before a difficult final shot. Going into Aichi-Nagoya, she had a clear target: gold.

The individual event at Aichi-Nagoya did not break her way. She finished 15th in qualification with 585 points after a difficult standing series, missing the individual final. But the team event offered a second chance, and India's trio used it. Chouksey's 585 combined with Tilottama Sen's 590 and Vidarsa Vinod's 588 to put India above Kazakhstan's 1,754 and claim silver.

## From an NCC Camp to the Asian Games

Ashi Chouksey's path to Aichi-Nagoya began not in a shooting range but in a school corridor. She was a student at Carmel Convent, BHEL, Bhopal, looking for something beyond routine classes when she joined the National Cadet Corps. At an NCC camp she picked up a rifle for the first time. She did not put it down.

By 2018 she had entered competitive shooting, training at the **MP State Shooting Academy in Bhopal**, which has produced a generation of national-level rifle shooters. Her progress was steady: World Cup finishes, three consecutive Asian Championship team golds (2023, 2024, 2025), and her first national individual title at the 67th National Shooting Championship in Bhopal. The 2022 Asian Games, where she won three medals in her debut, announced her as a shooter India would return to again and again.

The 2026 Games ended on October 4 with athletes connected to Madhya Pradesh having collectively brought home **eight medals** — two gold, four silver, and two bronze — across shooting, sailing, and hockey. Ashi Chouksey's contribution to that count was a second team silver. Four medals across two continental Games, all earned before she turns 25.

The next Asian Games are in 2030. She will be 28 and, if the trajectory holds, still aiming.
