Khandwa, Madhya Pradesh, has held a two-day celebration every October for 29 years: a music evening and a formal award ceremony for one exceptional figure from Indian cinema. This year's recipient is actor Raghubir Yadav. Madhya Pradesh's Department of Culture announced on 8 October 2026 that he will receive the **29th National Kishore Kumar Samman** at Police Ground, Khandwa, on the evening of 13 October — the death anniversary of the singer after whom the award is named.

Raghubir Yadav was born in 1957 in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh. He left home at fifteen, joined the Parsi Theatre Company, and eventually trained at the National School of Drama in New Delhi, where he specialised in stagecraft. He has since performed in more than **70 plays and approximately 2,500 shows** — a body of stage work that rarely makes headlines but forms the structural core of his craft.

## Three Oscar-Nominated Films and a Pradhan

Yadav's film debut came in Massey Sahib (1985), directed by Krishna Shah. He played the title role, an Indian Christian clerk caught between colonial administration and community loyalty. The performance earned him the **Silver Peacock Best Actor award** at the 11th International Film Festival of India in 1987 and the FIPRESCI Critics' Award at the Venice Film Festival in 1986. It was an extraordinary beginning.

He followed it with Salaam Bombay! (1988), directed by Mira Nair, playing Chillum, the drug-addicted street performer whose downward spiral the film watches without sentiment. The film was India's entry for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film. He appeared in two more Oscar-nominated films: Lagaan (2001, directed by Ashutosh Gowariker), where his comic timing as the village uncle balanced one of Hindi cinema's most serious subjects, and Water (2005, directed by Deepa Mehta). Three nominated films across two decades is a distinction shared by almost no one else in Indian cinema.

To the newest and largest generation of Indian viewers, Yadav is the Pradhan Ji of Panchayat — the well-meaning, flustered village head of the Amazon Prime series that has become one of Hindi-language streaming's most watched shows. The role brought him an audience that spans age groups and platforms.

The Kishore Kumar Samman has been awarded by the Madhya Pradesh government since 1997–98 and recognises work in direction, acting, screenplay writing or song lyrics. Raghubir Yadav will receive it in Khandwa, the hometown of Kishore Kumar himself, who was born there in 1929. A son of Madhya Pradesh honouring a son of Madhya Pradesh.
