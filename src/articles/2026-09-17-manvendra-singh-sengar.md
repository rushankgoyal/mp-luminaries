On National Engineers' Day — September 15, 2026 — Chief Minister Mohan Yadav presented the Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya Award to Manvendra Singh Sengar, Deputy General Manager of the Madhya Pradesh Building Development Corporation in Jabalpur.

The ceremony, held at Kushabhau Thakre Auditorium in Bhopal, was organised by the Public Works Department. Engineers, officials, joint teams, and construction agencies were honoured for outstanding contribution to Madhya Pradesh's infrastructure development. The PWD also signed a memorandum of understanding with CSIR-AMPRI (Council of Scientific and Industrial Research – Advanced Materials and Processes Research Institute), Bhopal at the same event — a collaboration designed to bring advanced construction materials research into the state's infrastructure pipeline.

Engineers' Day is observed on the birthday of Sir Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya, the civil engineer who designed the KRS dam in Karnataka, overhauled Hyderabad's flood defences, and became the first engineer to receive the Bharat Ratna, in 1955. The award bearing his name is the state's way of recognising engineers who embody that same tradition of meticulous, consequential work.

## Building the State's Public Infrastructure

The Madhya Pradesh Building Development Corporation is the agency that constructs and maintains the buildings where government functions: hospitals, district collectorates, court complexes, police buildings, school campuses, public works offices. Wherever a collector walks into a newly completed district headquarters or a patient enters a refurbished district hospital, the MPBDC's engineers have done the structural work behind it.

Sengar is posted in Jabalpur — MP's second-largest city and a major hub for the state judiciary, defence establishments, and central administration. As Deputy General Manager, he carries responsibility for project planning, quality assurance, and execution oversight across building works in his zone.

Presenting the awards, Chief Minister Yadav said that engineers are "key to MP's economic and social development" and that "their talent transforms development ideas into reality."

> "Engineers are key to MP's economic and social development. Their talent transforms development ideas into reality."
> — Chief Minister Mohan Yadav, Engineers' Day, September 15, 2026

The line captures what engineers actually do: they close the distance between a policy decision and a structure standing in the ground. The gap between an announced hospital and an operating one, between a government building sanctioned and a government building delivered — that gap is where engineers like Sengar work.

The PWD introduced the Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya Award alongside Rani Durgavati, Vikramaditya, and Raja Bhoj awards to make engineering excellence a visible, recognised career milestone in the state. Each award carves out a specific domain of achievement; together they signal that infrastructure work in Madhya Pradesh is something the state watches and rewards.

For Jabalpur and the communities whose public buildings have taken shape under Sengar's oversight, the recognition names someone doing essential, largely uncelebrated work. Madhya Pradesh's infrastructure programme advances one building at a time. Manvendra Singh Sengar has been making those buildings count.
