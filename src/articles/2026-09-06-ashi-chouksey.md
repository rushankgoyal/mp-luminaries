From the beginning, the rifle felt right. During an NCC training camp at Carmel Convent school in BHEL Colony, Bhopal, a young Ashi Chouksey held a rifle for the first time and put a first-attempt group on the target that made her instructor take notice. She was 13. Two years later, she was at the Madhya Pradesh State Shooting Academy, where the discipline is total and the lanes are measured in metres of accumulated pressure.

That early clarity of direction has translated, methodically, into one of the strongest résumés in Indian women's rifle shooting.

## Three Medals in Hangzhou, a Record in Uttarakhand

At the 2022 Asian Games — staged in Hangzhou in October 2023 — Chouksey arrived as a 21-year-old on her first major multi-sport appearance. She left with **three medals**.

She won **silver** as part of the Indian women's 10m air rifle team, silver again in the women's **50m rifle three positions** team event, and a **bronze** in the 50m rifle 3P individual final. No Indian woman in rifle shooting had returned from a single Asian Games with as many medals. For a Games that also included gold from the ISSF World Cup circuit — she had won a mixed-team gold in Baku, Azerbaijan, in 2022 — Hangzhou confirmed what Bhopal had been watching for years.

The national record came next, at the 38th National Games in Uttarakhand in early 2025. Competing in the women's 50m rifle three positions event, she scored **598** — surpassing the previous record of 594 that Sift Kaur Samra had held since 2023. The score placed her among the top contenders in Asia for the discipline she will compete in when the Games open in Japan.

Now 24 years old, Chouksey is in Bhopal this September, completing a final preparatory camp with the rifle and pistol squad before the Indian contingent flies to Aichi-Nagoya, where shooting events begin on **17 September**. She is in the squad for the women's 50m rifle three positions individual and team events.

> "I want to convert it into gold," she said recently, reflecting on the Hangzhou medals. "The experience, the training, the mental conditioning — everything is stronger now."

Sports psychology and biofeedback have become a deliberate part of her preparation. The target, in the most literal sense, is improvement of a few tenths on each shot. In precision shooting, that is the difference between a podium visit and a gold-medal ceremony.

Madhya Pradesh has a long tradition of producing rifle shooters — the state academy in Bhopal has fed the national programme for decades — but the depth of the current women's cohort is something new. Ashi Chouksey is the most visible result of that depth, a Bhopal athlete competing against the best in Asia with three Asian Games medals already in hand and a national record to build on.

The Asian Games open on **19 September 2026** in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.
